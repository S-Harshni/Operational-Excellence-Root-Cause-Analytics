Operations Root Cause Analytics

    How causes are assigned

    1. Payment approval delay: approval took more than 2 days. 2. Seller missed hand-off deadline: parcel reached the carrier after the seller's shipping limit. 3. Slow carrier transit: transit at least 1.5x and 3+ days longer than typical for that seller-state to customer-state route. 4. Promise shorter than the route needs: promised days below the route's typical delivery time. 5. Other.
